HEALTH CONCERNS

Understanding potential health concerns associated with excessive aluminium exposure.

← Back to Home Dashboard

Brain & Memory

Researchers continue to study the possible effects of excessive aluminium exposure on brain health and memory.

Bone Health

High levels of aluminium may interfere with normal bone mineralization and strength.

Kidney Stress

The kidneys help remove aluminium from the body. Excessive exposure may increase the burden on kidney function.

Vulnerable Groups

Children, older adults, and people with kidney-related health conditions may be more sensitive to aluminium exposure.
