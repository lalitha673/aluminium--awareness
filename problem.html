Cooking in Aluminium

Cooking frequently in aluminium cookware may increase exposure, especially when preparing acidic foods.

Acidic Foods

Tomato-based dishes, lemon, and vinegar can react with aluminium and increase migration into food.

Lack of Awareness

Many people are unaware of the potential health and environmental concerns related to excessive aluminium usage.
